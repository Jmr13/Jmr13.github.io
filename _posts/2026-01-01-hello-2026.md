Hello 2026! 

Our plan of hosting a blog website at the start of the year miserably failed, because my assumptions and collective knowledge surpasses reality. I've had all the hopes and expectations in mind for this project.

Hosting a blog site in Termux unfortunately cannot be achieved. There are no port forwarding available in mobile data plans, which is only available in cabled internet services. 

In addition, despite the existence of tunnel services, the URL to be generated is inconsistent and will continuously changes when restarting. We also steer away from paid hosting services to avoid any surprising recurring charges I'm too anxious to think about. 

This is our last resort on starting a blog site. May this experience not be a catalyst to an unforeseen circumstances this year.

God be with ye (Goodbye)
