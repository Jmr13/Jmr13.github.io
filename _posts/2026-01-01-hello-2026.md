Hello, 2026!

Our plan to host a blog website at the start of the year has miserably failed. It seems my assumptions and collective knowledge were no match for reality. I had so many hopes and expectations for this project, only to find myself confronting the limitations of what I could actually accomplish.

Hosting a blog site through Termux, unfortunately, turned out to be far more difficult than anticipated. What seemed like a simple technical challenge gradually became a series of roadblocks. Mobile data plans generally do not provide the kind of public IP access or inbound connectivity needed to host a publicly accessible website directly, unlike some home internet connections that may offer port forwarding.

In addition, although tunneling services exist to work around these limitations, their generated URLs can be inconsistent, especially when using temporary tunnels that assign new addresses after restarting. We also decided to steer clear of paid hosting services to avoid unexpected recurring charges.

This is our last resort in trying to start a blog site. This is what happens when ambition runs ahead of preparation, when the excitement of building something overshadows the practical realities of keeping it alive. May this experience serve as a lesson rather than a catalyst for further unforeseen circumstances this year.

God be with ye (Goodbye)
